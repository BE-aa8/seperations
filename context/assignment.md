# AI Project Assignment (verbatim from course materials)

AI Projects must have the following components:

1. Publicly accessible website demo/documentation
   Among many free options are: Molab, Streamlit.io, HuggingFace ZeroGPU Spaces, GitHub pages, Netlify, etc.
2. YouTube video of presentation - include as a link in the project website
3. Verbatim YouTube auto-generated transcript of the presentation - viewable section of the project website
4. Presentation/Documentation
   Overview. Demo what you/AI created. Make it clear that it is an AI project exploring AI capabilities. Explain the process, i.e., AI models, local/cloud services, prompts, etc., used to create it. Focus on the planning, iteration steps where you provided guidance and your reasoning. Explain key technical details of content related to this course and what you learned and what you want to learn more of in the future. Attribution/references.
5. Projects should compare the outcomes using different AI models.
   After generating a plan from one model, have a different AI model critique and suggest improvements. Justify your decisions on what to accept/reject. Implement the plan using at least 2 different AI models and compare.
6. Attribution/references, and what each group member contributed.

## Rubric
- Resourcefulness, curiosity, creativity: 25%
- Artifacts (Plan/Comparison, Webapp, Powerpoint): 25%
- Presentation: 25%
- Q&A: 25%

## Grading weight (from syllabus)
- Group AI Project: 20 points (up to 4 per submission/presentation), **or**
- Individual AI Project with recorded in-class presentation and detailed technical Q&A: 50 points
- Course maximum: 220 points

## AI policy (from syllabus)
- Use of AI is strongly encouraged, except during recording of HW video submissions and quizzes.
- Chat history/prompts are required for the AI project.
