Teaching point

Fractional stages are not linear

The final partial stage follows the geometric stage recursion and is therefore obtained from the logarithmic Kremser relation.

Teaching point

A below 1 can still be feasible

Feasibility is controlled by the two endpoint driving forces, not by an arbitrary A ≥ 1 rule.

Teaching point

HETP connects the descriptions

HETP = H_OG·A ln(A)/(A−1), with the balanced limit HETP = H_OG at A = 1.
