5. Educational scope

This MVP is an educational model, not a process-design package. It uses dilute compositions, constant molar flow, a straight equilibrium line, equilibrium stages with one overall tray efficiency, and constant H_OG. The SO₂ preset is intentionally labelled as a model-limitation example.

6. Future extensions

The plan identifies curved-equilibrium support, diameter/flooding calculations, pressure-drop comparisons, and a tray-versus-packing scenario exercise as future work. They are not part of this MVP.
