What to try

  • Drag the liquid rate down (the lower-right point, to the right) until the minimum-L/V flag appears. Watch the stage count run away. Note that the absorption factor A drops below 1 on the way — that is normal, not an error.
  • Click “Balanced (A = 1)”. The transfer-unit area becomes a rectangle, the packed composition profile becomes a straight line, and N = NOG = 4 exactly. Then click “Near-balanced” — the numbers should barely move.
  • Change the packing from Pall rings to structured. The number of transfer units does not change; only the height does. That is the difference between how much separation is needed and how efficiently the hardware delivers it.
  • Switch to the SO₂ preset and read the warning. It is in the list precisely because the straight-line model strains there.
